🔎 Analysis Process

1. Context

Start with the broader timeframe and determine whether the market is trending or ranging.

2. Structure

Mark important highs and lows. Look for higher highs/lows or lower highs/lows.

3. Levels

Identify important support and resistance zones.

4. Setup

Wait for your predefined conditions rather than entering because price simply moved.

5. Risk

Determine invalidation, stop-loss, position size and potential reward before entry.

6. Execute

Only act when every condition in your strategy is satisfied.

🎯 Example BUY Framework

  1. Market context is bullish.
  2. Price approaches a previously identified support zone.
  3. Your confirmation condition occurs.
  4. Entry is defined.
  5. Stop-loss is placed at a logical invalidation point.
  6. Target is defined before entry.
  7. Risk is within the strategy's limit.

🎯 Example SELL Framework

  1. Market context is bearish.
  2. Price approaches resistance.
  3. Your confirmation condition occurs.
  4. Entry is defined.
  5. Stop-loss is placed at logical invalidation.
  6. Target is defined.
  7. Risk remains within the strategy limit.

🧠 What Not To Do

❌ Don't chase a move.
❌ Don't increase risk to recover losses.
❌ Don't trade without a stop/invalidation plan.
❌ Don't treat indicators as guaranteed predictions.
Educational only: The examples are frameworks for learning, not guaranteed trading signals or personalized financial advice.
