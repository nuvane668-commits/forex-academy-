Support & Resistance

Support and resistance are areas where price has previously reacted.

Support

Support is an area where buying interest has previously helped prevent or slow a downward move.

Resistance

Resistance is an area where selling interest has previously helped prevent or slow an upward move.

Important: Think of support and resistance as zones rather than perfectly precise lines.

How to mark a zone

  1. Look for repeated price reactions.
  2. Identify the area containing important highs or lows.
  3. Mark a reasonable zone around the reaction area.
  4. Wait to see how price behaves when it returns.

Break and retest

Sometimes price breaks through an important zone and later returns to test it. This can become part of a strategy, but it is not guaranteed to work every time.
