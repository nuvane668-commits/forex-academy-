Finding Potential Entries

A good trading process starts with a plan. The goal is not to predict every market movement but to define situations where your strategy gives you a reason to participate.

Before considering an entry

  1. Check the broader market context.
  2. Identify the current market structure.
  3. Mark important support and resistance zones.
  4. Wait for price to reach an area relevant to your strategy.
  5. Look for your predefined confirmation condition.
  6. Define the invalidation point.
  7. Calculate the position size.
  8. Determine the potential target.

Example framework

The market is showing a bullish structure.
Price pulls back toward a previously identified support zone.
Your predefined confirmation condition appears.
You calculate the risk before considering the entry.

What not to do

Do not enter simply because a candle looks exciting, because price has already moved a lot, or because you are afraid of missing the move.
