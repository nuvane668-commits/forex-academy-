Trading Psychology

Trading decisions can be affected by emotions because outcomes are uncertain.

Fear

Fear can cause someone to exit too early, avoid valid setups or change their plan during a trade.

Greed

Greed can encourage excessive position sizes or unrealistic profit expectations.

FOMO

Fear of missing out can cause traders to enter after a large move has already happened.

Revenge trading

Trying to immediately recover a loss can lead to emotional decisions and excessive risk.

Discipline

A written trading plan can help reduce impulsive decisions. The objective is to follow a repeatable process rather than trying to win every trade.

Trading journal

Record why you entered, what your plan was, whether you followed it and what you learned.
