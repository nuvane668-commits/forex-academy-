Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis studies economic and financial factors that can influence currencies.

Important economic factors

  • Central-bank interest rates
  • Inflation
  • Employment data
  • Economic growth
  • Government policy
  • Geopolitical developments
  • Market expectations

Interest rates

Changes in interest-rate expectations can influence demand for currencies because they can affect the attractiveness of assets denominated in that currency.

Economic news

Major announcements can produce rapid price movements and changing trading conditions.

Beginner habit: Before trading, know whether major economic events are scheduled for the currencies you are analyzing.
