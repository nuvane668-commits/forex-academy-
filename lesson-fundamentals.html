LESSON 01

Fundamental Analysis

Learn how economic conditions, interest rates and major announcements can influence forex markets.

What Is Fundamental Analysis?

Fundamental analysis looks at economic and financial information that can influence the value of a currency. Traders study the economy behind the currency rather than relying only on the price chart.

Think of it this way:

The chart shows you what price is doing. Fundamental analysis helps you study some of the economic factors that may influence why market conditions change.

Important Economic Factors

Interest Rates

Central-bank decisions and expectations can influence currency demand.

Inflation

Inflation data can affect expectations about future monetary policy.

Employment

Employment reports can provide information about economic conditions.

Economic Growth

Growth data can change expectations about an economy.

Government Policy

Policy decisions can influence economic expectations and sentiment.

Geopolitical Events

Major events can create uncertainty and changing market conditions.

How to Read This Chart

Each candle represents a period of price movement. The candle body shows where price opened and closed, while the wick shows the higher and lower prices reached during that period.

Notice the difference:

Before the announcement, price is moving relatively gradually. Around the announcement, the example shows a much larger candle and faster movement. This demonstrates why important economic events can create changing trading conditions.

The Beginner's Habit

Before analyzing a currency pair, check whether important economic events are scheduled for the currencies involved. Knowing the economic calendar can help you understand when volatility may increase.
