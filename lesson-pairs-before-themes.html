Currency Pairs

A currency pair shows the value of one currency compared with another.

Base and quote currency

In EUR/USD, EUR is the base currency and USD is the quote currency.

Example:
EUR/USD = 1.1000

This means one euro corresponds to 1.1000 US dollars at that quoted rate.

Major pairs

Major currency pairs generally involve the US dollar and are among the most actively traded pairs.

Minor pairs

Minor pairs generally involve major currencies but exclude the US dollar.

Exotic pairs

Exotic pairs generally combine a major currency with a currency from a smaller or emerging economy. They can have different liquidity and trading costs.

Pips

A pip is a commonly used unit for describing small price changes in forex. The exact convention depends on the currency pair and broker.

Spread

The spread is the difference between the bid and ask price. It is one of the costs a trader should consider before entering a position.
