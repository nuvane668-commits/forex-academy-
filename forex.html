What Is Forex?

Forex, short for foreign exchange, is the global market where one currency is exchanged for another.

How does forex work?

Forex is traded in currency pairs. A pair compares the value of one currency against another.

Example: EUR/USD

EUR/USD compares the euro with the US dollar.

If EUR/USD is 1.1000, the quoted exchange rate means 1 euro corresponds to 1.1000 US dollars.

Why do currencies move?

Currency prices can change because of interest rates, economic data, inflation, political developments, market expectations and supply and demand.

Currency pairs

The first currency is the base currency; the second is the quote currency.

GBP/USD
GBP = base currency
USD = quote currency

Important beginner lesson

Forex is not a guaranteed way to make money. A trader can lose some or all of the money placed at risk. Risk management is therefore just as important as chart analysis.
