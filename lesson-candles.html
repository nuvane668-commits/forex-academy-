Candlesticks

Candlesticks are one of the most common ways traders visualize price movement.

Every candle can show:
Open — where the period began.
High — highest price reached.
Low — lowest price reached.
Close — where the period ended.

Candle body

The body shows the relationship between the opening and closing price.

Wicks

The upper and lower wicks show prices that were reached outside the candle body.

Bullish and bearish candles

A bullish candle closes above its open. A bearish candle closes below its open.

Do not trade candles alone

A candle pattern should be considered together with market structure, location, timeframe and risk management.
