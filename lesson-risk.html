Risk Management

Risk management is one of the most important skills a trader can develop.

Risk amount

A simple planning formula is:

Risk amount = Account balance × Risk percentage

For example, if someone chooses to risk 1% of a hypothetical $1,000 account, the planned risk would be $10. This is an educational example, not a recommendation of what percentage you should use.

Stop loss

A stop loss can define where the original trade idea is considered invalid. It does not guarantee the exact exit price in every market condition.

Risk/reward

Before entering, compare the amount you could lose with the potential amount you are targeting.

Position sizing

Position size should be calculated from the amount you are willing to risk and the distance to your invalidation point—not simply from how much money is available in the account.

Protect essential money

Never use money needed for school fees, rent, food, bills or emergencies as trading capital.
⚠️ Educational warning

Forex trading involves substantial risk. The examples on this website are for educational purposes and are not guaranteed trading signals or personalized financial advice.
