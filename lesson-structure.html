Market Structure

Market structure describes how price forms highs and lows.

Uptrend: higher highs and higher lows.

Downtrend: lower highs and lower lows.

Range: price moves between relatively defined areas without a sustained directional trend.

Why structure matters

Understanding structure helps a trader describe what price is doing instead of simply guessing whether it will rise or fall.

Break of structure

When price moves beyond an important previous high or low, it may provide information about changing market conditions. However, a break does not automatically mean a trade should be entered.

Multiple timeframes

A trader may use a higher timeframe to understand broader context and a lower timeframe to study a possible setup.

Beginner exercise

Look at a chart and mark the most recent significant highs and lows. Ask: Is price making higher highs, lower lows, or moving sideways?
